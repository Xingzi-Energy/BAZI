    Do I need my exact birth time?

    Your birth hour shapes the fourth Pillar, so an accurate time gives the most precise chart. An approximate hour still produces a useful reading; the minute helps us refine true solar time. If you only know a rough window, enter your best estimate.

    What is true solar time, and why does my birth city matter?

    Clock time and the sun's actual position can differ by 30 minutes or more depending on where you were born. We correct for your city's longitude, the equation of time, and local daylight-saving rules, so the chart is anchored to the sun's real position where you were born. This is the step that makes the Four Pillars calculation astronomically accurate.
